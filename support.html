Recoil Arena Support

Help with the iOS version, unlocks, and in-app purchases.

Contact support

For bugs, technical issues, or purchase-access problems, email bryandes@me.com.

It helps to include the device model, iOS version, game version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are welcome. Do not email your Apple password, credit-card details, or other account credentials.

Restoring purchases

If a previously purchased item appears locked, first confirm you are using the Apple Account used to buy it. Open Recoil Arena and use its Restore Purchases option. Make sure the device has an internet connection so Apple's purchase information can be checked.

Full Game ownership is required to access the optional Developer Support and Gore Pack content. Purchasing one optional pack does not automatically grant the others.

About the free version

Recoil Arena is free to download. Its opening rounds are playable before an optional one-time Full Game purchase is required to continue from the first boss encounter. Additional cosmetic and gore content is sold separately through Apple's in-app purchase system.

Refunds and billing

Billing, refunds, and purchase histories are managed by Apple. For an App Store refund request, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Gameplay progress and settings

Progress, unlocks, results, and preferences are stored on the device. The game's Reset Meta feature clears earned progression and records. Deleting or reinstalling the game may remove locally stored progress; some files may be included in device backups. Paid entitlements can be restored through Apple when available.

Privacy

Read the Recoil Arena Privacy Policy for information about local storage, Apple's purchase processing, and this support website.
