Privacy Policy

Recoil Arena for iOS

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cyber Titan Games ("we," "our") handles information in the iOS game Recoil Arena and on this Recoil Arena support website.

1. Information handled by the game

Recoil Arena does not require you to create an account. Based on the current iOS game implementation, it does not send personal information or gameplay records to a Cyber Titan Games server and does not incorporate third-party advertising or analytics services.

To make the game work, Recoil Arena stores gameplay-related information on your device. This can include preferences and settings, progress, earned unlocks, cosmetic selections, achievements and gameplay records. This information is stored locally within the app's files and is not transmitted to us by the game. Your device's operating-system backup services may include these local files depending on your settings.

2. In-app purchases

Recoil Arena offers optional one-time in-app purchases, including the Full Game, Developer Support cosmetics, and Gore Pack. Purchases and payment information are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not directly receive or store your payment card details.

The game receives purchase product information and verified entitlement or purchase status from Apple's system, allowing it to enable, restore, or disable purchased content. Apple's handling of transaction and account information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current version of Recoil Arena does not contain third-party ad networks, analytics SDKs, or behavior-based tracking services. We do not sell your personal information or use game activity for targeted advertising.

4. Website information

These support webpages do not include our own analytics scripts, advertising trackers, account sign-in, or information-submission forms. They are hosted using GitHub Pages, which may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the website. GitHub's practices are described in its Privacy Statement.

If you choose to contact us by email, we receive the information that you voluntarily include in your message. We use it only to respond to your inquiry and address related support issues. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game progress and settings remain on your device until changed or deleted through game functionality or operating-system app removal, subject to any device backups. Recoil Arena's Reset Meta option resets earned progression and records while retaining certain control preferences; it does not cancel or erase purchases recorded by Apple. You can restore eligible purchases through the game's Restore Purchases option.

If you email us, we retain correspondence only as reasonably necessary to answer and manage your request. You may ask us to delete that correspondence, subject to any applicable legal obligations. Apple controls the retention and handling of transaction records within its services.

6. Security and children

Game progress is stored within the app's local storage, subject to your device security and backup settings. The game does not have an account-creation feature and does not knowingly request personal information from children. The game's App Store age rating describes its intended audience.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app, its features, or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated wording and revision date on this page. If new advertising, analytics, or online services are added in the future, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be reviewed and updated as appropriate.
